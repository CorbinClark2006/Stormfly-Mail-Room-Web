Play without Safari's browser bars

iPhone Safari cannot make this game fullscreen inside a browser tab. Install the Home Screen version instead:

  1. Open this page in Safari, then tap Share (the square with an upward arrow). Depending on your Safari layout, Share may be inside the menu.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen. Scroll through the actions if needed.
  3. If shown, leave Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add.
  4. Leave Safari and open Mail Room from your Home Screen. Turn your phone sideways and tap Play.

The Home Screen version opens without Safari's address and tab bars. Internet is still required. Opening it starts a new game session.
